# Retrieval approaches: illustrative output comparison

2026-10-12. Same inputs throughout: NVDA, Grow my investment value, profitable_growth, long (>10 years), medium risk. Same frozen quarter/source cutoff. These are edited design examples based on manual review, not model runs or evidence of comparative quality. Retrieval changes what evidence is usable, not the underlying facts; good outputs can converge.

## Full structured report
What happened: NVIDIA reports Rubin racks running at named partners and management describes broader AI demand. Current operating profit and operating cash flow grew; the period-labeled cash-flow statement shows receivables and inventories absorbing operating cash.
Meaning: Deployment is a current execution milestone, not proof of repeat demand across product cycles. Receivable collection and inventory conversion matter to whether earnings become sustained cash generation over the long holding period. Medium risk does not change evidence strength or imply quantified risk suitability.
Unanswered/watch: Do partners expand/repeat deployments, and are receivables collected/inventories converted without persistent cash pressure? No claim of customer payment failure or inventory obsolescence. The report alone does not establish long-term durability or valuation.

## Section/table-aware retrieval
What happened: Read full product/deployment section and full cash-flow statement with period headers, units, reconciliation rows and notes.
Meaning: Same business implication as the full report, with inspectable current-quarter working-capital context. Fixes the current lookup limitation rather than inventing a distinct investment conclusion.
Unanswered/watch: Determine whether cash absorption is temporary growth funding or persistent collection/inventory pressure; follow comparable disclosures. Additional narrative explanation may remain absent even with complete numerical table context.

## Semantic + keyword retrieval
What happened: Search by meaning for repeat customer demand, product execution and sustained cash generation; return the partner milestone and complete cash-flow evidence if successfully retrieved.
Meaning: Same facts and long-horizon implication. Semantic retrieval could connect terminology to the question but is not inherently more accurate and cannot guarantee retrieval of repeat-order evidence.
Unanswered/watch: Need repeat-purchase and comparable cash evidence. Same single quarter means no actual cross-quarter trend claim; broader multi-filing indexing is separate scope.

## Adaptive investigation
What happened: Initial deployment/cash findings trigger a bounded request for full cash-flow context when returned rows omit headings.
Meaning: Expanded evidence resolves the period-label gap, but cannot alone explain underlying collection/inventory causes or prove repeat demand. Gap classification distinguishes source structure, absent explanation and future uncertainty.
Unanswered/watch: Carry forward repeat-order and collection/inventory questions, stopping when approved sources are exhausted or call/token limits reached. No new investigation claimed by these samples. A full long-term investigation may need additional approved filings, not just another read of this release.

## Recommendation

Start with full structured release analysis and exact in-packet citations, preserving tables/units/periods/sections. Let model reasoning prioritize relevance; code owns arithmetic and verification. Expose section/table reads for focused inspection when needed. Do not add semantic storage for one small source or a mandatory adaptive loop before evidence completeness is tested. Retain focus/full-context comparison to establish user benefit, including high/context settings as evaluated candidates rather than silently changing the current low/original baseline.

Evaluate missing-context resolution, materiality, exact source support, concrete goal mechanism, useful follow-up, uncertainty semantics, latency/cost and input sensitivity. More elaborate output is not automatically better. Recommendations are architecture proposals pending implementation, not approval of additional source scope.

Current actual low/original assessment: “Current profitability supported; durability remains untested.” Supports current revenue/operating-profit growth, operating-cash-flow growth and management's accelerating-demand statement; lacks a specific repeat-deployment mechanism and a complete question-specific cash reconciliation. Alternative examples above are illustrative, not measured retrieval outputs.

Preferred proposed follow-up: user selects an unresolved question; child run inherits all original inputs, cutoff and evidence IDs, requests at most two additional complete sections/tables from the approved packet, then returns evidence-assessment changes and residual uncertainty. A complete cash table can establish period-specific receivable/inventory cash absorption, not its underlying cause or future persistence. Missing source headings are ingestion defects to fix automatically; they should not be a marketed research capability or mandatory user chore. See PRD D51 for boundaries.
