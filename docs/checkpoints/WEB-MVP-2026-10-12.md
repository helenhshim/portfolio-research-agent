## Evaluation limits and next tasks

This demonstrates a working protected workflow, not general correctness, firm compliance or superiority to ChatGPT. Quote/reference checks are mechanical; interpretations were inspected only for this limited case. A stochastic successful retest does not prove the initial failure's cause or a zero-failure rate. Comparative removal can omit useful material and should be evaluated alongside shown-claim accuracy. Income versus saved growth examples changes several context variables; it does not isolate every input's effect.

Next: have an advisor explain this holding from the brief, measure checking/editing time and material omissions, then perform a matched actual ChatGPT task. Separately validate buy-side premise-checking. Improve payment reconciliation and source citation completeness where cases expose gaps. Public paid release requires access/spend controls; runtime SEC refresh requires aggregate acquisition controls. Persistence/adaptive investigation remains the next agentic continuation rather than a fake MVP control.
